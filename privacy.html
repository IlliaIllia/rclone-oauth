Privacy policy

  • This OAuth client is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to store encrypted backups in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • It requests only the drive.file scope, so it can see only the files it created itself.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared or sent anywhere other than the owner's Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
